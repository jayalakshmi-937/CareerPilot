CAMPUS PLACEMENT POLICY & RULES (BATCH OF 2026)
Reference ID: PL-2026-REG
Last Updated: August 2025

1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Students must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 6.0 to participate in the campus placement drives. Individual companies may specify a higher threshold (typically 7.0 or 7.5 CGPA).
- A student must have no active backlogs at the time of registration for a placement drive. Pre-existing backlogs that have been cleared are not counted.
- Students must have a minimum aggregate of 60% in Class X and Class XII examinations.

2. REGISTRATION RULES
- Registration for each company placement drive is mandatory through the Student Placement Portal.
- The registration window opens exactly 5 days before the scheduled drive and closes 48 hours prior to the test/interview. Late registrations will not be entertained.

3. JOB OFFER POLICIES
- One Student, One Job Policy: Once a student receives an offer from a company, they are automatically deregistered from all subsequent placement drives, with the exception of the "Dream Offer" policy.
- Dream Offer Policy: A student who has obtained an offer with a CTC (Cost to Company) below 8.0 LPA is eligible to apply for a "Dream Offer" company, provided the dream company offers a CTC that is at least 1.5 times the current offer or is above 12.0 LPA. A student can only secure one Dream Offer.
- Super Dream Offer: A student with an offer above 12.0 LPA can apply for "Super Dream" companies offering CTC greater than 20.0 LPA.

4. INTERVIEW CODE OF CONDUCT & DRESS CODE
- Attendance is compulsory for all rounds once a student registers and passes the initial resume screening. Failing to report for a scheduled test or interview without 24 hours prior medical notice will result in a 3-week suspension from all placement activities.
- Dress Code: Strict business formal attire is required. 
  - For male students: Solid light-colored long-sleeve shirts, dark trousers, necktie, and polished black leather shoes.
  - For female students: Professional formal suits, blazers, or formal Indian wear (sarees/salwar suits of sober colors) with formal footwear.
