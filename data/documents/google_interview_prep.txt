GOOGLE SOFTWARE ENGINEER INTERVIEW ROADMAP
Company: Google LLC
Roles: Software Engineer (L3/L4 - University Graduate)

Google's selection process for university graduates comprises resume shortlisting, online coding assessments, and multiple rounds of technical interviews.

1. SCREENING & ROUNDS
- Online Coding Challenge (Google Online Challenge - GOC): 2 algorithmic coding questions (60 minutes).
- Technical Phone Screen (1 round, 45 minutes): Coding and algorithms on a shared doc.
- Onsite Rounds (3 to 4 rounds, 45 minutes each):
  - 3 Coding & Algorithm Rounds: Deep focus on algorithms, complexity analysis, data structures, and edge cases.
  - 1 Googliness & Leadership Round: Behavioral assessment focusing on teamwork, adaptability, dealing with ambiguity, and ethical decision-making.

2. CORE TOPICS TO PREPARE
- Data Structures: Graphs (DFS, BFS, Dijkstra, MST, Topological Sort), Trees (Binary Trees, BST, Trie, Segment Trees), Hash Tables, Stacks, Queues, Heaps.
- Algorithms: Recursion, Dynamic Programming (Memoization and Tabulation), Divide and Conquer, Greedy Algorithms, Binary Search, Sliding Window, Two Pointers.
- Complexity: Big-O analysis for time and space is mandatory for every solution discussed. Students must explain optimal vs. suboptimal trade-offs.

3. KEY GOOGLINESS BEHAVIORS
- intellectual humility, bias for action, supporting team members, navigating ambiguous situations, and respecting diversity.
