RESUME BUILDING STANDARDS & GUIDELINES
Category: Career Development Center

A student resume for campus placements must be concise, accurate, and professional. It must fit on a single A4 page.

1. STANDARD STRUCTURE & SECTIONS (Top to Bottom)
- Contact Information: Full Name, Professional Email, Phone Number, LinkedIn Profile, GitHub Link. Do not include photo, address, or date of birth.
- Education: Table format listing University/Degree, Year of Graduation, CGPA/Percentage. Also list Class XII and Class X school, year, and percentage.
- Technical Skills: Grouped by category:
  - Programming Languages: e.g., Python, C++, Java, JavaScript.
  - Databases & Cloud: e.g., PostgreSQL, MongoDB, AWS, Firebase.
  - Tools & Frameworks: e.g., Git, Streamlit, React, Node.js.
- Academic & Personal Projects: List 2-3 projects. Include title, technologies used, and 2-3 bullet points describing what you built, how you built it, and the impact (quantified if possible).
- Internships & Experience (if any): Company, role, duration, and key contributions.
- Achievements & Extracurriculars: Competitive programming ratings, scholarship awards, hackathon wins, student club leadership positions.

2. CRITICAL DOS AND DON'Ts
- Do use strong action verbs (e.g., "Developed", "Optimized", "Designed", "Led", "Integrated").
- Do quantify your achievements (e.g., "Optimized database queries, reducing API response times by 35%").
- Don't exceed one page.
- Don't include spelling or grammatical errors.
- Don't list outdated soft skills like "Hardworking" or "Punctual" without supporting context.
