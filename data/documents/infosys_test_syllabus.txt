INFOSYS PLACEMENT SYLLABUS & SELECTION PROCESS
Company: Infosys Limited
Roles: System Engineer (SE), Specialist Programmer (SP), Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)

1. RECRUITMENT ROUNDS
- Round 1: Online Assessment (Cognitive & Technical Test)
- Round 2: Technical Interview (Coding, core CS fundamentals, resume projects)
- Round 3: HR / Behavioral Interview

2. ONLINE ASSESSMENT PATTERN FOR SYSTEM ENGINEER (SE)
The online exam lasts for approximately 100 minutes and consists of five core sections:
- Reasoning Ability (15 questions, 25 minutes): Analytical reasoning, data interpretation, logic puzzles.
- Mathematical Ability (10 questions, 35 minutes): Permutation & combination, probability, series, analytical geometry, algebra.
- Verbal Ability (20 questions, 20 minutes): Cryptarithmetic, sentence completion, reading comprehension.
- Pseudo-code (5 questions, 10 minutes): Direct questions asking to trace output of code snippets in C/C++ style, covering logic, loops, pointers, and memory layout.
- Puzzle Solving (4 questions, 10 minutes): Grid-based puzzles, logical deduction.

3. SPECIALIST PROGRAMMER (SP) & DSE ROUNDS
For SP and DSE roles, the online assessment is purely coding-focused:
- Duration: 180 minutes.
- Structure: 3 programming problems of varying difficulty (Easy-Medium, Medium-Hard, Hard).
- Language support: Java, Python, C++, C.
- Topics: Dynamic Programming, Graph Algorithms, Greedy Strategy, Segment Trees, and String Algorithms.
